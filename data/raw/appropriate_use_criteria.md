# Intended population
Blood biomarker testing is intended for adults with cognitive symptoms who have been evaluated by a clinician. Testing in cognitively unimpaired people is not supported by current appropriate-use criteria, and asymptomatic screening should not be offered outside research settings.

# Performance thresholds
Under this policy, an assay may be used for triage when sensitivity is at least 90 percent and specificity is at least 75 percent. An assay may be used as a confirmatory test when both sensitivity and specificity are at least 90 percent.

# Setting of use
In primary care a blood test is used for triage: a positive result should be followed by confirmation through specialist evaluation. In specialty memory clinics, a high-performing assay can serve as a confirmatory test when it is integrated with the clinical picture.

# Documentation
The clinician should document the indication, the assay used and the interpretation of the result, and should discuss the meaning of the result with the patient.
