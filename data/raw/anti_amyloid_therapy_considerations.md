# Eligibility
Anti-amyloid antibody therapies are considered for people with mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia due to Alzheimer's disease. Confirmation of amyloid pathology by amyloid PET or CSF analysis is required before treatment.

# Safety monitoring
Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA) are the main safety concern and include edema and microhemorrhage. Patients need a baseline MRI and periodic MRI monitoring during treatment.

# Genetic risk
People who carry two copies of the APOE e4 allele have a higher risk of ARIA, so APOE genotyping is typically discussed before treatment starts and the result is counseled carefully.

# Anticoagulants
Use of anticoagulant medication requires special consideration because of bleeding risk.
