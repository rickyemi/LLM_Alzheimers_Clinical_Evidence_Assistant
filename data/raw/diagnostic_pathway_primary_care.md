# Initial assessment
Start with a history from the patient and an informant, a medication review and a brief cognitive screen. Rule out reversible contributors such as depression, B12 deficiency, thyroid disease, sleep disorders and medication effects.

# When to refer
Refer to a memory clinic or specialist when symptoms are progressive, when the presentation is atypical, when the patient is younger, or when a biomarker test result is positive or intermediate.

# Role of blood testing
In primary care a blood biomarker test may be used as a triage step to decide who should be referred for confirmation. A negative result in a symptomatic patient makes Alzheimer's disease pathology less likely and prompts a search for other causes.

# Shared decision making
Discuss the purpose, the possible outcomes and the implications before testing, including implications for planning and for eligibility for treatment.
