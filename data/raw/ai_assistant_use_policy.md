# Purpose and scope
The AD Clinical Evidence Assistant provides decision support to qualified clinicians and medical affairs staff. It summarizes published guidance and internal evidence with citations.

# Prohibited uses
The assistant must not diagnose individuals, recommend individual dosing, or interpret a named patient's results. Requests of this kind are refused and routed to a human reviewer.

# Human oversight
Every output is reviewed by a clinician before it influences care. The assistant displays its sources and the corpus version with each answer.

# Change control
Any change to the model, prompt, corpus, threshold or tool configuration is a controlled change. It must pass the golden-set regression gate before release, and the AI review board signs off.

# Incident handling
Suspected unsafe outputs are reported through the incident runbook within one business day and are recorded in the audit trail.
