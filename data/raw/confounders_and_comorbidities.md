# Chronic kidney disease
Reduced kidney function can raise plasma concentrations of several biomarkers, including NfL and, to a lesser degree, p-tau markers. Interpretation in patients with chronic kidney disease requires caution and may call for confirmatory testing. Ratio-based measures tend to be less affected than single-analyte concentrations.

# Body mass index
Higher body mass index is associated with lower measured plasma biomarker concentrations through a dilution effect.

# Age and other conditions
Biomarker levels can differ with age. Co-existing neurological conditions and vascular disease can complicate interpretation, and mixed pathology is common in older adults.

# Representation and subgroup performance
Performance should be checked across age groups, sexes and ancestry groups, because many validation cohorts under-represent some populations. Subgroup performance gaps must be reported and monitored after release.
